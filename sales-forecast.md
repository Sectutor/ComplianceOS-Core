## Key Assumptions

1. **Market Size**: ~500K SMBs needing SOC 2/ISO 27001 annually
2. **TAM Capture**: 0.17% by Year 3
3. **Churn Rate**: 5% annually (industry standard: 5-7%)
4. **Expansion Revenue**: 10% of customers upgrade annually
5. **Sales Cycle**: 30-60 days for SMB, 60-90 for Enterprise
