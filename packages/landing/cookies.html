Cookie Policy

Effective Date: February 18, 2026

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help websites run efficiently and provide information to site owners. At GRCompliance, we use cookies primarily for security and platform stability.

2. How We Use Cookies

We categorize our cookies into three types:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Essential for logging in, navigating the platform, and maintaining secure sessions. These cannot be disabled.

Functional Cookies

Used to remember your preferences (like language or UI themes) to provide a better user experience.

Performance & Analytics

Help us understand how users interact with our marketing site so we can optimize our vetting and onboarding flows.

3. Controlling Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. However, disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent you from accessing the application portal. You can reset your cookie preferences on our site at any time via the cookie banner.

4. Updates to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our technology or regulatory requirements. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
