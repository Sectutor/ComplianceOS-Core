# ISO 27001:2022 Implementation Plan Template

## Phase 1: Plan (Months 1-3)
**Focus**: Preparation, gap analysis, and planning (Clauses 4-6).

### Step 1: Gain Management Support and Form a Team
- **Activities**: Secure executive buy-in, appoint project leader, define roles.
- **Deliverables**: Executive commitment letter, Team charter (RACI), Project plan.
- **Duration**: 2-4 weeks.
- **Tips**: Use cybersecurity report to justify resources. Involve C-level early.

### Step 2: Understand the Standard and Perform Gap Analysis
- **Activities**: Review ISO 27001:2022 structure (Clauses 0-10, Annex A). Assess current policies.
- **Deliverables**: Gap analysis report, Context documentation.
- **Duration**: 4-6 weeks.
- **Tips**: Prioritize high-impact gaps.

### Step 3: Define ISMS Scope and Risk Methodology
- **Activities**: Outline assets, locations, processes. Choose risk assessment method.
- **Deliverables**: Scope statement, Risk assessment methodology.
- **Duration**: 2-4 weeks.
- **Tips**: Limit scope initially to critical areas.

### Step 4: Conduct Initial Risk Assessment
- **Activities**: Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities. Evaluate risks.
- **Deliverables**: Risk register, Preliminary risk treatment options.
- **Duration**: 4-6 weeks.
- **Tips**: Focus on likelihood and impact.

## Phase 2: Do (Months 4-7)
**Focus**: Implementation (Clauses 7-8).

### Step 5: Develop Policies and Procedures
- **Activities**: Create mandatory docs (ISP, Access Control). Select Annex A controls.
- **Deliverables**: policies, SoA, Risk Treatment Plan.
- **Duration**: 4-8 weeks.
- **Tips**: Use templates. Align with business objectives.

### Step 6: Implement Controls and Processes
- **Activities**: Deploy governance, people, physical, and tech measures.
- **Deliverables**: Implemented controls (firewalls, etc.), Operational procedures.
- **Duration**: 6-8 weeks.
- **Tips**: Document evidence of implementation.

### Step 7: Launch Awareness and Training Programs
- **Activities**: Train staff, role-specific training.
- **Deliverables**: Training materials, Attendance records.
- **Duration**: 2-4 weeks (ongoing).
- **Tips**: Use quizzes. Schedule annual refreshers.

## Phase 3: Check (Months 8-9)
**Focus**: Effectiveness evaluation (Clause 9).

### Step 8: Conduct Internal Audit
- **Activities**: Plan and perform audit. Gather evidence.
- **Deliverables**: Audit plan, Audit report.
- **Duration**: 4-6 weeks.
- **Tips**: Use impartial auditors.

### Step 9: Perform Management Review
- **Activities**: Review ISMS performance and risks with leadership.
- **Deliverables**: Management review minutes.
- **Duration**: 1-2 weeks.
- **Tips**: Discuss audit results.

## Phase 4: Act (Months 10-12)
**Focus**: Improvement and certification (Clause 10).

### Step 10: Address Non-Conformities and Improvements
- **Activities**: Root cause analysis, corrective actions.
- **Deliverables**: Corrective action plans.
- **Duration**: 2-4 weeks.
- **Tips**: Use "Five Whys".

### Step 11: Prepare for Certification Audit
- **Activities**: Select certification body, pre-audit checks.
- **Deliverables**: Audit-ready documentation.
- **Duration**: 4-6 weeks.
- **Tips**: Engage auditors early.

### Step 12: Achieve Certification and Maintain
- **Activities**: External audit, address findings.
- **Deliverables**: ISO 27001 certificate.
- **Duration**: 2-4 weeks + ongoing.
- **Tips**: Plan for recertification every 3 years.
