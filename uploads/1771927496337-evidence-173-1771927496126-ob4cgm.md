# Risk Management Strategy Template

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this strategy is to establish the organization's approach to managing risks to organizational operations, assets, and individuals.

## 2. Scope
This strategy applies to all information systems and data within the organization's boundary.

## 3. Risk Tolerance
[Define the organization's acceptable level of risk, e.g., Low, Moderate, High]

## 4. Assessment Methodology
Risks will be assessed using the NIST SP 800-30 Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments.

## 5. Mitigation Strategy
Risks exceeding the tolerance level must be mitigated, transferred, or accepted by the Authorizing Official.
