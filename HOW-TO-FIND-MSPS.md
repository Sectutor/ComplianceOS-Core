# HOW TO FIND MSPs (Managed Service Providers)

*Practical ways to find compliance MSPs and vCISOs*

---

## WHO ARE THE TARGETS?

### Primary Targets:
| Target | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **vCISOs** | Virtual CISOs - fractional security leaders |
| **Compliance Consultants** | Firms doing SOC 2, ISO 27001 audits |
| **MSPs with security practice** | IT managed services adding compliance |
| **GRC Firms** | Governance, risk, compliance consultancies |
| **MSSP** | Managed security service providers |

### What They Care About:
- Managing multiple client compliance
- White-label (their brand, not yours)
- Pricing they can resell
- Easy to use
- No competition with their clients

---

## METHOD 1: LINKEDIN OUTREACH (Best ROI)

### Steps:
1. Search: "vCISO" OR "compliance consultant" OR "SOC 2" in your area
2. Look at their profiles - do they manage client compliance?
3. Connect with personalized message

### Templates:

**Connection Request:**
```
Hi [Name], I help MSPs and compliance consultants manage unlimited clients with their own branding. 

Would love to show you how some firms are using it to scale from 10 to 100+ clients.

Happy to hop on a 10-min call or send a quick demo.

[Your name]
```

**Follow-up (if no response in 5 days):**
```
Hey [Name], wanted to bump this - still happy to show a quick demo if helpful.

If not a fit no worries at all.

Best, [name]
```

### Daily Goal: 20 connection requests → 5 responses

---

## METHOD 2: COLD EMAIL (Scale)

### Finding Emails:
- Use Apollo.io or Hunter.io
- Search: "compliance consultant" + "company"
- Or search specific companies

### Email Template:

**Subject: [Company] + unlimited client compliance?**

```
Hi [First Name],

I'm reaching out because we've helped [similar company] cut compliance costs by 80% while serving 3x more clients.

Here's the pitch:
- Your own branding (not ours)
- Unlimited clients, unlimited users
- Self-hosted or cloud
- They pay you, you pay us

Quick question: How many compliance clients do you manage today?

If it makes sense to talk, I'm happy to hop on a 15-min call.

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, ComplianceOS
```

### Send 50-100/day using cold email tools
